01 / About

A researcher who builds.

I am a PhD scholar in Computer Science at the Indraprastha Institute of Information Technology, Delhi, working on Neurosymbolic AI: combining the pattern-finding power of deep learning with the rigour of symbolic reasoning. My work covers formal methods (SMT solving with Z3 and model checking), ontologies and the verification of large language models, along with efficient LLM inference on NVIDIA Triton.

Before the PhD I spent several years building production AI. At Chadura Tech I built AI pipelines, chatbots and recommendation systems. At Tata Consultancy Services I worked on DevSecOps and intelligent automation. As a Junior Research Fellow at IIT Patna I built an AI proof of concept for a coal power plant, and at IIT Indore I developed acoustic early-warning models that run on edge devices. I hold an M.Tech in Data Science & AI from Mahindra University and a B.Tech in Information Technology from SRM University, where I graduated with First Class with Distinction.

Alongside research I edit books for CRC Press (Taylor & Francis) and IOP Publishing, and I review for Springer's Scientific Reports and Discover Artificial Intelligence. I also write technical courses and articles. Away from the keyboard, I write poetry and paint.

02 / Research

Teaching machines to learn and reason.

My research sits where deep learning meets logic. I focus on AI that can explain itself and can be checked.

Neurosymbolic AI

Hybrid systems that pair neural perception with symbolic knowledge and logic, so that AI is both capable and interpretable. This is the core of my PhD at IIIT-Delhi.

  • PhD research
  • Reasoning
  • Hybrid AI

LLM Verification & Model Checking

Using formal methods, SMT solvers such as Z3 and model checking to test whether large language models behave as intended, towards AI that people can trust.

  • Formal methods
  • SMT · Z3
  • LLMs
  • Trustworthy AI

Ontology & Knowledge Graphs

Structuring domain knowledge as ontologies and graphs, so models can ground their answers in facts that are explicit and can be inspected.

  • Ontology
  • Knowledge graphs
  • Semantics

Explainable AI

Making model decisions understandable to the people who rely on them. I edited the CRC Press volume Explainable AI (XAI) for Sustainable Development.

  • XAI
  • Interpretability
  • CRC Press

Acoustic & Edge AI

H-MTL and domain-adaptation models for an acoustic early-warning system, built at IIT Indore to run on edge devices in changing field conditions.

  • Edge AI
  • Audio
  • Domain adaptation

Applied AI for Industry

At IIT Patna I built an AI proof of concept that tunes control-room parameters at a coal power plant. At Launchpad.ai I built satellite-image segmentation microservices.

  • Energy
  • Computer vision
  • MLOps
